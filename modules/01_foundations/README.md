# Module 1: Foundational Paradigm & Governance

## 1.1 Confronting the Probabilistic Black-Box Myth
For years, the artificial intelligence industry has operated under a single dominant assumption: Large Language Models are probabilistic black boxes that predict the next token based on rolling probabilities, and the only way to guide them is through prompt engineering, temperature tuning, or post-hoc validation retry loops. 

Non-Stochastic AI (NSAI) shatters this assumption. Instead of treating generation as a game of chance where you cross your fingers and hope the model doesn't hallucinate, NSAI treats token generation as an engineering boundary problem. By intercepting logit vectors at runtime, we can enforce strict mathematical certainty.
